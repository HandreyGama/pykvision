THE TEN COMMANDMENTS.

BY: HANDREY GAMA.

BEFORE DOING ANYTHING, READ THIS. EVERYDAY OF YOUR LIFE.

    1 . YOU SHALL COMMIT TO SIMPLICITY AND FUNCIONALITY.

    2 . EVERYTHING MUST BE DOCUMENTED, INCLUDING THE DOCUMENTATION ITSELF.

    3 . IF YOUR CODE NEEDS COMMENTS TO EXPLAIN WHAT IT DOES, THEN IT ISNT GOOD CODE.

    4 . IN DISCUSSIONS ABOUT TOPICS, BE TOLERANT AND FRIENDLY, BUT DO NOT BE COMPLACENT.

    5 . DO NOT TRY TO SOLVE A PROBLEM THAT DOES NOT EXIST.

    6 . WHENEVER YOUR BUILD A FEATURE, AIM TO CREATE SOMETHING THAT IS EASY TO MAINTAIN, AND SCALABLE.

    7 . NEVER SAY SOMETHING IS IMPOSSIBLE ALWAYS ASSUME THE PROBLEM IS A SKILL ISSUE.

    8 . BEFORE PERFECTING SOMETHING, FIRST MAKE IT WORK.

    9 . LEARN TO FIND BALANCE BETWEEN THINGS. 

    10 . NO TICKET? NO PROBLEM.

REPEAT THIS 0X0A TIMES.
